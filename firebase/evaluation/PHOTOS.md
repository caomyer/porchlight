## What was seen (2026-11-24, `gpt-image-2.6-flare`)

- Every pet was recognisably itself in all three tries: coat colour, markings, the calico's patches and collar, the black cat's eye colour.
- The three moods kept the same character in every case, and each animation held the character still across its nine frames while it blinked and moved its tail.
- No call was refused and none failed: 26 calls, 26 drawn.
- The cut-out of the dalmatian brought the kneeling person's legs along, and one of its three tries drew them into the character, where every mood then kept them.
  The first drawing's prompt now says to draw only the one animal and leave out anything else in the picture; on a second run none of the three tries drew them.
- The style is a clean illustrated sticker with a pixel edge, smoother than strict pixel art.
- A whole character measured 34 cents with one animation, which makes about 55 cents with all four.
