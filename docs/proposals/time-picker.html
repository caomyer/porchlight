A time picker that looks like the rest of the wall

The meal and walk forms use the system's time picker: a gray box that opens an iOS wheel. It works, but it looks borrowed. This proposal draws the time as one of the app's own pills, and tapping it opens a ruler of the day right in the form, with the usual meal times shaded so the time and the meal make sense together.
