Proposal · Photo frame

Choose what the idle wall shows

The photo frame is where the wall spends most of the day. Today it shows your photos if you've added any, otherwise a painted sky, and you can't pick. This adds one choice, and makes the sky the restful option.

The ask "Can we further optimize the photo frame? Have a setting to control whether to use the default wallpaper or randomly show the photos."
One choice: our photos, shuffled, or the porch view. The sky holds still and changes only with the time of day, so the wall barely works at all. Photos keep their slow drift, in a shuffled order.

Try it

Switch the setting on the left. The wall on the right shows what you'd see when nobody has touched it for 10 minutes. In Porch view, drag the time to watch the sky follow the day.

Build notes

  • New setting ambient.show (photos | sky) in AmbientSettings, read by the Settings row and AmbientView.
  • Photos: a shuffled play order rebuilt each time it runs out, so no repeats until every photo has shown. The drift and fades stay in Core Animation as now.
  • Sky: picks the sky from the hour (Morning 5-11, Day 11-4, Dusk 4-7, Evening 7 until night), painted once into an image and shown still. The existing once-a-minute clock tick decides when to fade to the next one.
  • Settings: the "Show" switch sits at the top of the Photo frame group, with no previews. Under Our photos, one line counts your photos and "Manage" opens a sheet: every photo in a scrolling grid, newest first, with Add, and Select to tap several and remove them in one go. The picker already takes up to 30 photos at a time. The count line, Manage and "Add photos" appear only with Our photos.
  • Tests: a unit test for the shuffle and the hour-to-sky rule; a UI test that switches to Porch view and sees the photo frame follow. Then re-measure on the iPad with scripts/idle-cpu.sh to replace the estimate.
