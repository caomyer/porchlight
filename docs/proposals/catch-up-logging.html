Catching up on care

"Forgot to log feeding her last night, and now I can't find anywhere to add it." The app has nowhere to add a care log for a day that has passed. Every new walk or feeding goes on today.

One rule, no new controls

A missed walk or feeding shows as a faint mark where its record would be. Tap it to log it for that day. This uses the places that already show care records, and the log sheet you already know.

This already happens for today: a walk that's still due shows as a dashed mark. The change just keeps that mark on earlier days instead of hiding it. Try both views below. It's 8:10 AM on Thursday, and Wednesday's dinner was never logged.

Decisions

QuestionRecommendation
Calendar, companion page, or both?Both. They come from the same list of care records, so both get the marks from one change. The calendar covers every pet and every routine. The companion page is where people go to look at one pet.
How far back are missed slots shown?This week only, the same range as Paw trail and the diary. Older weeks just show what was logged.
What if a whole day was skipped, like a trip?Show the marks anyway. They're faint and they don't nag. If this turns out to be noisy, we can hide the marks for a day where nothing was logged at all.
Stars and streaksSame as on time, the same as chore catch-ups. The streak fills back in.
CelebrationA short confirmation instead of the full-screen celebration: "Dinner logged for Wednesday · +1 star".
Not coveredAdding an extra walk on a day where every slot was already logged, or moving a log saved on the wrong day. Both are rare. Fixing a log keeps working as it does now.

Build notes

  • Saving already works for any date. Only the sheet limits it, because it works out the day from today or from the log being fixed. The care log request gets an optional day and slot, and the sheet uses them instead.
  • CompanionTimeline.items already adds expected slots for today. It also adds missed slots this week, as items that open the sheet for that day. The calendar and the diary read from it. Today's agenda shows only today, so it doesn't change.
  • Paw trail's missed dots become buttons.
