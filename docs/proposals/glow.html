Proposal - Glow on the phone and the wall

Glow should answer "what did we do for each other this week?"

Today the phone's Glow is one number, an empty reward card and two bars. The wall already knows far more than the phone shows. This proposal gives both screens the same story, in each one's own shape, and adds the one thing neither has: a way to look back.

What Glow is forWhat is thin todayPhoneWallBuild orderDecisions

What people come to Glow for

1. To be seen

"I walked the dog five times this week." Stars are a thank-you. The screen should say what the stars were for, not only how many.

2. To see the load

For two adults: who is carrying what, without a scoreboard. For kids: pride in their own week against their own last week.

3. To look forward

What are we saving for, how close are we, and when will we get there at this pace.

4. To look back

Last week, the week before, the rewards we already enjoyed. The same wish you raised for the paw trail.

Rule kept from the first design: no winner, no ranking words, nothing that loops or pulses. People sit side by side, each compared with their own last week.

Cashing in becomes a family moment

Today the wall spends the stars on one tap with no question and no celebration. Proposed: the wall asks once, naming who earned the round, then celebrates across the whole screen. When someone cashes in from a phone, the wall celebrates too the next time it is looked at.

Same words on both

Every line (trend, pace, "x4", who did what) comes from one place in the code, so the phone and the wall can never disagree about a week.

Build order

1. The phone catches up
  • Household total, your share, trend
  • Week strip by person
  • Where the stars came from
  • Lately
  • Person page

Mostly drawing what the wall already computes.

2. Looking back
  • Week pager on phone and wall
  • Cashed-in history
  • The wall's per-task lines and tap on a person

Needs star history loaded further back than today's three weeks. No change to what iCloud stores.

3. Rewards
  • Pace line
  • One-tap reward ideas
  • The wall asks and celebrates, also for a cash-in made on a phone

Each step ships alone through TestFlight. Nothing here needs a new iCloud record type, so there is no Console step for you.

Decisions for you

  1. Lately on the phone: yes?The first phone design left the feed on the wall only. I now think the phone needs it: it is where "what just happened" is asked most. Recommended: yes.
  2. People side by side, never ranked: keep?Each person is compared with their own last week. No "most stars" wording, no leader. Recommended: keep. A household with kids may later want a gentle "streak" instead.
  3. The pace line: "about 3 days away at this pace"?It uses the last two weeks' average and says nothing when there is too little to go on. Recommended: yes.
  4. One-tap reward ideas when nothing is being saved for?Your own screen shows this state today. Three ideas plus "Your own", editable afterwards. Recommended: yes.
  5. Should anything else earn stars?Today only chores and pet or plant care do. Cooking a home-cooked meal is the obvious candidate. Recommended: not now; it can be added as a chore already.
