Porchlight · Time field · Proposal for approval

A time you can just type

One small field for every time in the app. At rest it's a pill showing the time. Tap it and a small card opens from the pill: a few suggestions for the usual cases, and a number pad to type any other time. The iPad's own keyboard never comes up, so nothing on the screen moves or gets covered. A start and end is simply two of them.

Try it
Clock

Tap the keys, or type on a keyboard: a hardware keyboard on the iPad types straight in too. Done or Return keeps the time; tapping elsewhere does too; Escape cancels.

The rules
Type it the way you'd say it

7 is 7:00, 715 is 7:15, 1930 is 19:30. Add a or p if you like.

The right half of the day

Without AM or PM, a log takes the half of the day nearest the time it shows (so 8 on a breakfast is 8 AM), never later than now. An event takes the next one, and an end the first time after its start. The AM | PM switch fixes the rare miss.

A few suggestions

Up to four, fitted to the job: "Just got back" and "1 hr ago" for a walk, "Usual dinner" for a meal, lengths for an event's end.

Its own number pad

Twelve big keys in a card by the time. The iPad's keyboard would cover half the wall and hide fields low in a form; the card covers nothing but itself.

Ranges behave like a calendar

Moving an event's start moves its end. An end before its start is the next day, marked +1. Settings like the night keep their end.

Mistakes are explained

A time a log can't have, like later than now, gets a short reason under the field. Nothing changes silently.

The iPad's clock

12 or 24-hour follows the iPad's setting. On a 24-hour clock there is no AM | PM switch at all.
