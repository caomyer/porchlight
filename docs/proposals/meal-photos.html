Meal photos: shoot, keep editing, add more later

Today the shutter saves the meal on the spot, a saved meal can never get a photo, and the photo library only appears when there is no camera. This proposal turns photos into a tray you fill before saving and can add to any time after, with several photos per dish, while the wall keeps showing one picture per dish.

iPad Pro 10.5", landscapeMocks drawn at 1112 x 834 ptNov 9, 2026
What changes

Shutter saves. Tapping it stores the meal with whatever the form held, so the name, eaters and rating are skipped.

The shutter only takes a photo. It lands in a tray under the camera, the camera stays live for another angle, and the meal saves when you tap Save.

No photos after saving. The meal detail and its edit form have no way to add, swap or remove a photo.

The meal detail gets an Add photo tile (camera or Photos). Edit lets you remove photos and pick the cover.

Photos library is a fallback. It shows only when the camera is missing or turned off.

A Photos button sits beside the shutter at all times and picks several at once.

One photo per meal. A second angle or the dessert plate has nowhere to go.

Up to 6 photos per dish. The first is the cover; any photo can be made the cover.

The rule that keeps the wall readable

One dish, one polaroid. Photos live inside it.

A meal slot can already hold several dishes, shown as paper corners behind the polaroid and swiped through sideways in the week grid. Photos must not compete with that. So every place that shows many meals at once shows each dish's cover only, with a small camera count when it has more. Paging through a dish's photos happens in one place, the meal detail, where sideways swipes have no other meaning.

How it would be built
  • A new journalEntryPhoto table (entry, file, thumbnail, order). A migration moves each meal's current photoFile into it as photo 1, then drops the old columns.
  • Photos save to disk when they enter the tray, and the meal row with its photo rows saves in one transaction on Save. Discarded tray photos are deleted at once; the existing sweep catches anything left after a crash.
  • Thumbnails stay the only images aggregated views load, so grids stay as fast as today.
  • UI tests: shoot, then fill the form and save; add a photo to a saved meal; pick from Photos; change the cover; the week grid still swipes dishes.

Not in this change: video, cropping or filters, and photos on chores or companion logs.
