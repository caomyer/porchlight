Porchlight · Hours · Proposal for approval

The same ruler for a stretch of hours

Meal and care times now use the app's pill and day ruler. Two places still show the system's gray boxes, and both pick a stretch of hours rather than one time: a routine's windows (Morning 6 to 10am) in the companion editor, and the night hours in Settings. This proposal gives them the same pills and the same ruler, with two handles, one for each end.

Today and proposed

Today: two system boxes

Morning6:00 AMto10:00 AM

Each box opens its own wheel, so the two ends are picked blind to each other and to the routine's other windows.

Proposed: two pills, one ruler

Morning6:00AMto10:00AM

Tapping either pill opens one ruler below the row, with that end's handle active. The stretch between the handles is shaded.
