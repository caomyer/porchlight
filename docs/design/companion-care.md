# Companion care: who is credited, and earlier weeks

Two rules for logging a companion's care and reading it back, from what James saw on his own devices in November 2026.

## Who is credited

A log names the people who did it, and nothing guesses for them where it can't know.

- On a phone, a new log starts with the phone's own person, as it always has: the person holding it is the one most likely to have walked.
- On the wall, a new log starts with nobody when the household has two or more people, and with the one person when there is only one.
  The wall can't know who is standing at it, and a guess credits the wrong person whenever the other one walked and nobody looks.
- While nobody is picked, Save can't be pressed and says what it waits for, in the routine's own words: "Pick who walked", "Pick who fed", "Pick who did it".
  The sheet's title already asks the question ("Who walked Yo-Yo?").
- The same holds wherever the wall opens the log sheet: Today's card, a companion's page, catching up on a missed slot, and Pitch in's care tasks.
- Fixing a log keeps its own people.
- On a phone's Today card, "Also went" belongs to the lead routine it is labelled for: the big button credits the phone's person and whoever is picked there, then clears the picks; a smaller button for another routine credits the phone's person alone and leaves the picks for the lead.
- Fixing who did it changes only the people who changed: a person still on the log keeps their row untouched, so fixing a log's time sends the log alone.

## Earlier weeks

A companion's page, on the phone and on the wall, pages back through earlier weeks with the same pager as Food's diary (`WeekPager`, `WeekPaging`): seven-day steps, this week ending today.

- It goes back to the week of the companion's arrival or its first log, whichever is earlier, and comes back to this week when the day turns.
- On an earlier week, the paw trail, the numbers (walks, average, slots on time, the business log or stars), the diary and the podium are that week's.
  The count's and stars' labels say "that week" instead of "this week".
- The streak is today's on any week, and on an earlier week its label says so: "streak today".
- A logged slot still opens its log to fix.
- A slot that wasn't logged offers to catch up only within the catch-up days (`CareCatchUp.daysBack`, the last six days), as it does this week; older ones are only shown.
- Today's slots, the mood, the "Log a walk" button and the milestones are about now, on any week.
